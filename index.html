How to read this tool

  • Jobs, openings, and wages are subregional. The source dataset reports one set of labor market figures per SOC code covering the three-county Monterey Bay Subregion. County-level demand and wage estimates are not present in the data, so selecting a county filters the training programs and narrows the occupation list — it does not rescale the demand or wage figures.
  • 2027 Jobs is a projected employment level; Avg. Annual Openings combines growth and replacement need and is usually the more useful planning number for program capacity.
  • Median annual wage is calculated as median hourly × 2,080 hours. It is a full-time-equivalent figure and will overstate earnings in occupations with substantial seasonal or part-time work.
  • Sector and group totals sum each occupation once. An occupation belongs to exactly one CDE sector and one major occupational group in this dataset, so totals do not double count.
  • “No local program” means no provider in this dataset maps a program to that occupation. It is a signal to verify, not proof that no training exists.
  • Two data notes worth a look. The provider list contains both “Monseby College” and “Monsbey College,” which appear to be the same institution spelled two ways. Separately, four occupations (Phlebotomists, Nursing Assistants, Massage Therapists, Medical Assistants) carry more than one SOC code in the source sheet with identical demand figures; this tool uses the Centers of Excellence mapping as canonical and counts each occupation once.
  • Source and attribution. Occupational projections, wage estimates, and entry education come from the Bay Region Center of Excellence for Labor Market Research, part of the California Community Colleges Centers of Excellence network. The Monterey Bay Subregion shown here (Santa Cruz, Monterey, and San Benito counties) is one of the Bay Region’s five economic subregions. Program inventory is joined locally. This navigator is not an official COE publication — for published reports and dashboards, see COE data tools. Occupations shown: 382. Program records: 3,472 across 27 listed providers. “Unclassified Occupation” (SOC 99-9999) is excluded.
